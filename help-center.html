Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I update my account information? +

Open Account Settings and select Personal Information to update your account details.

How can I check my order status? +

Open the Orders section from your account to view your order status and order details.

Which payment methods are supported? +

Available payment methods are shown during checkout and inside your Payment Methods section.

How can I find a product? +

Use the search bar or browse product categories to find products available on SmartBazaar.

How can I keep my account secure? +

Use a strong password and keep your account information private. Never share your login credentials.

What is SmartBazaar? +

SmartBazaar is an online platform designed to provide products, services and digital experiences through one convenient account.
